## What this site collects, and why

This is a static website. It has no accounts, no logins and no forms of its own. It collects information in three ways.

**Analytics.** Google Analytics 4 (loaded through Google Tag Manager), Vercel Web Analytics and Microsoft Clarity record how the site is used: pages viewed, time on page, scroll depth, approximate location from your IP address, device and browser type, and the page that referred you. We use this to understand what gets read and to fix what doesn't work. Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies.

**Marketing tags.** The LinkedIn Insight Tag, HubSpot tracking code and Apollo website-visitor tracking may set cookies or read your IP address to recognise returning visitors, to identify the company a business visitor is browsing from, and to measure whether LinkedIn campaigns lead to visits and bookings. Under some privacy laws this counts as "sharing" personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

**Booking and email.** If you book a call, you do so on Calendly, whose [privacy policy](https://calendly.com/privacy) applies to what you enter there; booking links from this site carry campaign parameters that tell us which page you came from. If you email us, we keep the correspondence for as long as the conversation and any engagement lasts.

## Consent and your choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics and marketing cookies are set only after you allow them in the banner (lawful basis: consent). Elsewhere they are set by default (lawful basis: our legitimate interest in understanding and marketing this site) and you can decline them. Either way, the **Cookie settings** link in the footer reopens the banner so you can change your choice at any time, and withdrawing is as easy as allowing. Declining is also how to opt out of the "sharing" described above; you can also use the Global Privacy Control signal in your browser, which we honour as an opt-out. You can additionally block cookies in your browser settings.
